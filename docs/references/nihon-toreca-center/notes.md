# Nihon Toreca Center Reference Notes

## Overall Impression
Nihon Toreca Center is a major Japanese online oripa platform with a bright, commercial, and highly conversion-focused UI. It emphasizes pack variety, campaign banners, point-based pulls, prize pools, app promotion, free demo pulls, and repeat usage.

This service is useful as a reference for understanding the business flow of Japanese oripa platforms. However, the visual style feels very Japanese, promotional, and gacha-like. Pull Hub should borrow the structure and mechanics, but should not copy the visual tone directly. The final Pull Hub UI should feel cleaner, more premium, more transparent, and more suitable for a US trading card audience.

## Elements to Borrow
- Clear category navigation for different card types and product types
- Sidebar navigation for oripa, rankings, shop, and exchange features
- Filter chips for pack types, rarity, tags, bonuses, and conditions
- Strong pack discovery structure on the home page
- Campaign and featured pack sections that guide users toward active offers
- Pack detail pages with large promotional image, remaining quantity, and progress indicators
- Prize pool layout that shows reward tiers and available items
- Point / coin-based purchase and pull flow
- Free demo pull flow for first-time users
- Confirmation modal before opening a demo pack
- Result screen showing the pulled card, grade, card details, and coin value
- Repeat-action flow that encourages users to pull again or register

## Elements to Avoid
- Overly bright and noisy promotional banners
- Excessive rainbow, gold, neon, and jackpot-style visual language
- Anime/gacha-style characters as the main brand tone
- Too many competing colors on the same screen
- UI that feels cheap, crowded, or aggressive
- Heavy use of urgency and hype
- Japanese game-center or pachinko-like atmosphere
- Making the product feel like gambling rather than a transparent collectible experience
- Overloading the home page with too many campaigns at once

## How to Apply to Pull Hub
Use Nihon Toreca Center mainly as a reference for business flow and conversion structure. Pull Hub should borrow the way it organizes pack categories, filters, campaign packs, prize pools, point-based pulls, demo pulls, confirmation modals, result screens, and repeat actions.

Do not copy Nihon Toreca Center’s visual style directly. Pull Hub should avoid the bright Japanese gacha feeling and instead combine this business structure with Phygitals-style premium design and Clove-style pack-opening flow. The final Pull Hub experience should feel more credible, more spacious, more transparent, and more appropriate for the US market.
